AI Systems Principles

What I optimize for when AI enters the system.

The site should make the engineering judgment clear: AI is useful, but production systems still need authority boundaries, tests, rollback paths, and measured behavior.

Deterministic authority

Keep critical business logic outside the model. Let AI explain and assist, not invent authoritative values.

Least-privilege tools

Use bounded tool surfaces, explicit allowlists, budget limits, and safe fallbacks when model behavior is unsafe.

Retrieval grounding

Make answers traceable to source evidence, with clear boundaries between facts, summaries, and recommendations.

Regression evals

Turn prompt failures, guardrail bypasses, and integration bugs into permanent tests instead of tribal memory.
